🔒 Privacy Policy

Effective: June 27, 2026 Site: word-le-answers.com

This Privacy Policy describes how word-le-answers.com ("we", "our", or "the site") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit our website. We are committed to being transparent about our data practices.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information (such as your name, email, or address) directly. However, third-party services we use may collect the following automatically:

  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website (how you found us)
  • General geographic location (country/city level, not precise)
  • Device type (desktop, mobile, tablet)

2. Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site — for example, which pages are most popular and how users navigate between pages. This helps us improve the site.

Google Analytics uses cookies to track anonymous usage data. Google may use this data in accordance with its own Privacy Policy. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

3. Google AdSense

We display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Google uses cookies and similar technologies to show you relevant ads based on your interests and browsing history.

  • Google may use the DoubleClick cookie to serve ads based on your prior visits to our site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising at Google Ads Settings.
  • For more information, see Google's Advertising & Privacy FAQ.

4. Cookies

Our site itself does not set cookies directly. However, third-party services (Google Analytics and Google AdSense) set cookies on your device. These cookies help us understand site traffic and allow Google to serve relevant ads.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some features.

5. Third-Party Data Sources

The Wordle answer data displayed on this site is sourced from publicly available information and the New York Times Wordle game. We do not collect or store any personal data from users in connection with Wordle gameplay.

6. Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

7. Links to Other Websites

Our site may contain links to third-party websites (such as nytimes.com). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will indicate the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
